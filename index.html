About Me.

I am a second-year undergraduate student at Tsinghua University, pursuing a dual-degree program in Mathematics and Physics and Software Engineering through Weiyang College.

I am currently conducting research at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, in Hao Zhao's research group. My research focuses on learning-based physical simulation, particularly neural operators for computational fluid dynamics. More broadly, I am interested in developing physically grounded models that connect machine learning, simulation, and embodied intelligence.

Research Interests.

01

Neural Operators & Scientific Machine Learning

Learning efficient surrogate models for complex physical systems, particularly fluid dynamics.

02

Geometry-Aware Physical Modeling

Modeling physical processes involving complex geometries, moving boundaries, and dynamic interactions.

03

Physical World Models for Robotics

Exploring how physical knowledge and learned dynamics can support prediction and interaction in embodied environments.
